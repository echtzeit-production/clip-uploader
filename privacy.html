Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Clip Uploader is a private tool used only with the operator's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from other people.

Data processed

  • The TikTok access token and open ID of the operator's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload.
  • The video files the operator chooses to upload.

Purpose and storage

The token is stored only locally on the operator's computer and is used solely to upload the operator's own videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. Videos are transferred only to TikTok.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the tool is no longer used.
